# International supply-chain rider (negotiation template)

For each order specify origin, destination, importer/exporter of record,
Incoterms® rule and named place/version, customs valuation, duties/taxes, title
and risk transfer, cargo insurance, inspection, restricted-party screening,
export classification/licence, end-user/end-use and diversion prohibition.

The supplier must identify manufacturing origin, serials, authentic-parts
channel, firmware provenance, warranty/service territory and controlled
components. Substitution, re-export, remote access from another jurisdiction
and shipment-route change require prior written approval and renewed screening.

Attach the applicable JPN, USA, mainland China, Hong Kong or EU Member-State
data/security schedule. Specify data locations, remote-support locations,
subprocessors, transfer mechanism, regulator/incident contacts, government-
request handling and return/deletion proof.

Governing law, forum, language precedence, currency/FX date, withholding/gross-
up, permanent-establishment allocation, local agent and mandatory-law overrides
must be completed by counsel and tax advisers for both sides.
